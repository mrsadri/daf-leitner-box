# Storyline: „Ein Foto aus Graz“

## Genre and tone

Warm-hearted ensemble drama with a gentle family mystery. The 30 lesson contexts follow newcomers through everyday life: first job, family, sport, flat-hunting, festivals, health, careers, travel, history, emigration and language. A slice-of-life tone fits that arc, and the mystery (who is “H.” on an old photo?) gives every story a reason to turn the page without leaving the everyday situations the lessons teach.

## Setting

Graz, Styria, Austria. The heroes live and work in Lend, Gries, Geidorf and the old town around the Schlossberg and the Uhrturm (clock tower). Graz stays home base. The lessons send them out on trips:
- story 3: Bern
- story 5: Munich during the Oktoberfest, then Vienna
- story 6: a chocolate factory in Eastern Styria and Lake Constance at Bregenz
- story 7: Vaduz in Liechtenstein and Hamburg
- story 8: an alp in Graubünden, Switzerland
- story 9: Dresden and Berlin
- story 10: Innsbruck

The series follows the book's seasons and runs for about three years. Story 1 starts in September of year 1. Story 5 jumps from spring to the autumn of year 2. Story 6 ends in the summer of year 3.

## Main characters

### Nilufar „Nili“ Karimi
- **Age and background:** 26, from Shiraz, Iran. She has a degree in cultural management and arrives in Graz for a one-year internship at Murklang, a small Graz company that organises concerts and exhibitions.
- **Personality:** Organised and curious. She is shy for the first five minutes, then very funny. She writes everything down in a small green notebook.
- **Goal:** To get a real job in Austria. She also wants to find out who “H.” is on an old photo of her grandfather Bahram, taken in Graz in May 1976.
- **Fixed visual description:** Nilufar Karimi, a 26-year-old Iranian woman, 1.62 m, slim build, oval face with warm olive skin, large dark-brown almond-shaped eyes, thick straight black eyebrows, a small beauty mark under her left eye, shoulder-length wavy black hair tied in a low ponytail with a few loose strands framing her face; she wears a teal knitted scarf, a navy-blue cardigan over a cream blouse, dark-grey trousers, small silver crescent-moon earrings, and carries a brown leather crossbody bag.

### Lucía Fernández
- **Age and background:** 22, from Seville, Spain. She has done one year of teacher training and comes to Graz as an au pair with the Steiner family.
- **Personality:** Loud, warm and spontaneous. She sings while she works, cooks for everyone and is fearless in German, though often wrong.
- **Goal:** To pass the entrance exam for music education at the Kunstuniversität Graz. She also wants to prove to her family that she can make it on her own.
- **Fixed visual description:** Lucía Fernández, a 22-year-old Spanish woman, 1.68 m, athletic build, round face with light tan skin and freckles across her nose and cheeks, hazel-green eyes, long thick curly dark-brown hair held back by a red fabric scrunchie, wide smile; she wears a light-blue denim jacket with three small enamel pins (a guitar, a lemon, a star) on the left chest, a white T-shirt, black jeans, white sneakers, and small gold hoop earrings.

### Jonas Huber
- **Age and background:** 29, born and raised in Graz. He is a graphic designer and event technician at Murklang. He plays bass in the rock band „Die Murbuam“ and runs half marathons.
- **Personality:** Calm, with a dry humour. He is extremely helpful and hopeless with appointments.
- **Goal:** To finish the Graz half marathon under 1:40. Secretly, he wants to open a small music café one day.
- **Fixed visual description:** Jonas Huber, a 29-year-old Austrian man, 1.88 m, tall and lanky, long narrow face with light skin, short messy ash-blond hair, light stubble, blue-grey eyes behind round tortoiseshell glasses; he wears an olive-green hooded sweatshirt under an open charcoal-grey wool jacket, dark-blue jeans, grey running shoes, a black digital wristwatch on his left wrist, and often carries a grey canvas backpack.

## Overall arc

Nili arrives in Graz with a photo. It shows her grandfather in front of the Uhrturm in 1976 with a young woman, and the back says only „Bahram und H., Graz, im Mai 1976“. Over ten stories, Nili, Lucía and Jonas grow from strangers into a found family. Each story is driven by the everyday situations of its three lessons. Clues about “H.” appear one at a time: a frightened look at Christmas, a note in a lost wallet, a fairy tale, an old letter that comes back after almost fifty years. H. turns out to be Hermine Steiner, who was Bahram’s German teacher and close friend before he went home in 1979. In the last story, Bahram comes to Graz. Nili has to decide where her own home is.

## Story synopses

### Story 1: Neu in Graz (Lessons 1 to 3)
In September, Nili starts her internship at Murklang. She meets the team, fills in forms and learns to spell her name for Austrians. At the evening German class she meets Lucía, an au pair from Seville. Lucía is living with the Steiner family and learning to wake children, cook and do the big weekly shop. On a free weekend the three of them explore Graz: the market, the cathedral, the Stadtpark. Nili shows them the 1976 photo of her grandfather and the mysterious “H.” **Hook:** Nili’s temporary room ends in October, and in November she will have no room any more. Jonas asks her whether she can run.

### Story 2: Laufen, suchen, waschen (Lessons 4 to 6)
Jonas pulls Nili and Lucía into his running club. They train for the Graz half marathon, and Jonas falls at kilometre 15 but finishes. At the same time, Nili searches desperately for a room. Thanks to Jonas, she finds one with Frau Pichler in Lend and furnishes it with flea-market furniture. A disastrous evening at the laundromat turns the band’s white T-shirts pink. **Hook:** Frau Lechner sends Nili and Jonas to a music fair in Bern.

### Story 3: Von Bern bis Weihnachten (Lessons 7 to 9)
In Bern, Nili and Jonas struggle with the ticket machine, find the clock tower and survive a Swiss barbecue invitation where they bring the hosts’ own wine as a gift. Nili gets lost and discovers the Zentrum Paul Klee. Back in Graz, Kemal’s inn teaches them Austrian food words, and a small birthday party for Lucía ends with spilled red wine. At Christmas the Steiners invite Nili and Jonas. Grandma Hermine sees Nili’s photo and goes pale. **Hook:** Hermine says only „Den Mann kenne ich“ and asks Nili to wait until after New Year.

### Story 4: Neue Wohnung, neue Regeln (Lessons 10 to 12)
Frau Pichler sells her house, and Lucía’s au-pair year ends. The two women find a flat in Geidorf with a view of the Schlossberg and move in with Jonas’s help. They agree on flat-share rules, Lucía starts at the university and writes home about feeling foreign, and a local radio station interviews them about the Styrian dialect. Lucía opens a bank account. That evening Nili’s wallet, with the photo inside, is stolen at a book reading, and she reports it to the police. Paul finds the wallet. Inside is a note: „Komm am Sonntag um drei. H.“ **Hook:** Hermine tells a fairy tale about a princess and a stranger with a pear seed, then stops before the ending.

### Story 5: Gesundheit, Herbst und Wien (Lessons 13 to 15)
Hermine falls ill with bronchitis. Nili and Lucía take her to the doctor, read the package leaflet and nurse her back to health. Recovered, Hermine admits that she is “H.”: she was Bahram’s German teacher and closest friend, and he never came back after summer 1979. Half a year later, the friends go to the Munich Oktoberfest, where Jonas’s band plays in a small tent. Then Nili and Jonas drive to Vienna, stay with a couchsurfing host and visit the university archive. **Hook:** In Bahram’s student file Nili finds Hermine’s letter from 1979 asking for his address, and a card with the reason her letters never arrived: the house number was wrong, 41 instead of 14.

### Story 6: Welcher Beruf? (Lessons 16 to 18)
Murklang cannot extend Nili’s contract. A career counsellor recommends a trainee programme, and Nili presents her career path in her German class. Jonas learns that he needs a business plan for his café loan. Lucía applies for a summer internship at a family chocolate factory in Eastern Styria, gets it and learns the company’s history. The friends camp at Lake Constance near Bregenz: the tent collapses in the rain, they sail, visit the island of Mainau and the Stone Age pile dwellings, and Nili has an interview in Liechtenstein. **Hook:** Nili gets the trainee job in Liechtenstein, and Jonas’s eyes are sad.

### Story 7: Zwischen Vaduz und Hamburg (Lessons 19 to 21)
Nili books her journey at a travel agency, learns about the principality, loses her suitcase at Zurich airport and writes a claim to the airline. She starts as a trainee in Schaan. In Graz, Jonas is hit by a car while cycling. He needs no operation, but there is an angry visit from the driver and an insurance claim to fill in. At the station, Jonas tells Nili that he thought only of her. Lucía visits a fellow student in Hamburg and sings in an amateur crime play. **Hook:** After the play, Hermine calls: a yellow envelope from the Austrian Post has arrived with her own letter to Bahram from 1979.

### Story 8: Post aus der Vergangenheit (Lessons 22 to 24)
The letter was found behind an old machine in a former post office. Nili and Hermine read it together. Nili sends a parcel with a copy and the photo to Shiraz. It gets stuck in customs, so she complains and asks for help in an internet forum. Jonas adopts a dog called Kafka. Lucía gives a talk on higher education and decides to stay in Graz for her master’s. For a university assignment she interviews people with unusual life paths and discovers that Hermine learned Persian for Bahram and became an interpreter. The three friends volunteer for a week on an alp in Graubünden. **Hook:** Back in reach of the internet, Nili finds a message from Bahram: he has read the letter and must tell her something about 1979.

### Story 9: Fehler, Verträge und Geschichte (Lessons 25 to 27)
In Liechtenstein, Nili makes etiquette mistakes with her new manager (a kiss on the cheek, “du” instead of “Sie”, a question about salaries) and turns them into a popular blog for German learners. A chip company in Dresden reads the blog and offers her a job. She signs the contract, while Jonas announces that his loan is approved and he will open the „Café Uhrturm“. Nili starts in Dresden in the Silicon Saxony cluster. On 9 November the friends meet in Berlin and walk through the city’s history and its many religions. On a video call, Bahram finally explains 1979: his father fell ill and died, he had to support his family, he could not leave the country after the revolution, and his own letters went to an old address. **Hook:** Bahram says, „Ich komme nach Österreich.“

### Story 10: Hier und dort (Lessons 28 to 30)
In Dresden, Nili presents a survey on why skilled workers emigrate and return, and she asks herself where her home is. Bahram flies to Munich. Nili and Jonas take him through Innsbruck to Graz, where he and Hermine meet again under the clock tower and talk about their lives and about democracy. Nili gives a talk on the German political system and watches a federal election night without being allowed to vote. At an “evening of languages” in Jonas’s café, Hermine and Bahram talk about German, Persian and the varieties of German, and Nili announces a job at the University of Graz. On New Year’s Eve, friends read poems, Bahram and Hermine announce a trip to Shiraz together, and Nili writes on the back of a new photo: „Bahram und Hermine. Graz, im Mai. Endlich.“
