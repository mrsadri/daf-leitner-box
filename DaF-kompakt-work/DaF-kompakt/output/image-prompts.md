For Gemini image generation. One prompt per sequence; each prompt is self-contained.

- **Nilufar:** Nilufar Karimi, a 26-year-old Iranian woman, 1.62 m, slim build, oval face with warm olive skin, large dark-brown almond-shaped eyes, thick straight black eyebrows, a small beauty mark under her left eye, shoulder-length wavy black hair tied in a low ponytail with a few loose strands framing her face; she wears a teal knitted scarf, a navy-blue cardigan over a cream blouse, dark-grey trousers, small silver crescent-moon earrings, and carries a brown leather crossbody bag.
